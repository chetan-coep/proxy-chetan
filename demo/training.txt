Tokenization is the process of converting text into smaller units called tokens.

Natural language processing uses tokenization to prepare text for machine learning models.

Byte pair encoding is a subword tokenization algorithm that repeatedly merges the most frequent adjacent token pairs.

Language models learn patterns from text using tokens and their numerical representations.

The tokenizer should support words, punctuation, whitespace, numbers, programming languages and special characters.

Byte pair encoding builds a vocabulary by learning frequent combinations from a training dataset.

A good tokenizer should preserve the original text during encoding and decoding.
